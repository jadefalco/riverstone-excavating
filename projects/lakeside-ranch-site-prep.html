Featured Project

Lakeside Ranch
Site Prep

A complete site preparation of a 12-acre lakeside ranch, transforming dense brush and uneven terrain into a build-ready property.

Location

Okanagan Lake

Scope

Land Clearing & Site Grading

Features

Brush removal, stump grinding, rough grading, access road construction

Equipment

Excavator, Bulldozer, Skid Steer

Project Overview

A rugged lakeside property cleared and graded for future development.

The property was heavily covered in sagebrush, scattered timber, and rocky outcrops, with no established access from the road.

Our team cleared the land, ground stumps, constructed a gravel access road, and graded the building envelope to exact elevations for the client's future ranch home.

Land Clearing

Clearing the way for construction.

The clearing phase removed brush and timber while protecting the mature trees the client wanted to preserve along the property line.

Selective clearing preserved the natural tree line while opening up the build site and driveway corridor.

Grading & Access

Built for drainage and durability.

Rough grading established proper drainage away from the build site, while a gravel access road provided all-weather entry for construction traffic.

Final grades were set to engineer specifications, ensuring water sheds away from the foundation and future driveway.

The Result

A build-ready lakeside ranch.

The site was delivered clean, properly graded, and ready for the foundation contractor. What began as overgrown, inaccessible terrain is now a prepared canvas for the client's dream ranch.
