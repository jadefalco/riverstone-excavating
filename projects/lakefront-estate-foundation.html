Featured Project

Lakefront Estate
Foundation

A precision foundation excavation on a steep Okanagan lakeside lot, including rock breaking and drainage integration.

Location

Okanagan Lake

Scope

Foundation Excavation & Rock Work

Features

Rock breaking, footing excavation, perimeter drainage

Equipment

Excavator with Hydraulic Hammer

Project Overview

Precision excavation on a challenging lakeside slope.

Set into a hillside above Okanagan Lake, this custom estate required exacting foundation cuts through compact soil and bedrock.

We excavated full-height basement walls, installed footing trenches, and integrated a perimeter drainage system to manage groundwater on the slope.

Rock-breaking was required along the lake-facing wall, where bedrock sat just below grade. The result was a clean, accurately dimensioned foundation ready for formwork.

Foundation Cut

Clean walls and accurate depths for a complex foundation.

Tight dimensional tolerances and careful benching allowed the foundation crew to set forms directly against our cuts.

Excavated walls were within tolerance and free of loose material, allowing the forming crew to proceed immediately.

Rock Breaking

Power through bedrock without compromising the site.

A hydraulic hammer attachment broke through exposed bedrock along the foundation perimeter, creating clean lines and proper footing bearing.

Drainage

Integrated water management for a lakeside slope.

Perimeter drainage and strategic grading direct water away from the foundation and toward approved discharge points.

Final grades protect the foundation while maintaining the natural drainage patterns of the hillside.

The Result

A foundation ready for formwork.

The site was delivered with clean excavation lines, rock removed to bearing, and drainage installed — allowing the builder to move directly into forming and concrete placement.
